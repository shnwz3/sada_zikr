Sada Zikr Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

This policy covers the Sada Zikr Android app (com.sadazikr.app), published by Shnwz.

Data we collect

None. Sada Zikr does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, such as reminder times, selected adhkar, volume and theme, are saved only on your device. The home-screen widget reads these same on-device settings. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your account. We cannot access that backup.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders: to time reminders.
  • Run at start-up: to restore reminders after a restart.
  • Vibration, foreground service and wake lock: to play a reminder and let it finish.

Sharing and links

If you tap Share or Rate, Android opens the app you choose or Google Play. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes its data from your device.

Children

Sada Zikr collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
